About

A design leader who builds teams, strategy, and products in equal measure.

Over more than twelve years, I've moved between the drawing board and the leadership table — shaping enterprise platforms, AI-powered solutions, and customer-facing products for some of the world's most recognized organizations. My work sits where business strategy, user needs, and technical reality meet.

Today, as an Experience Design Manager at EPAM Systems, I lead UX strategy across global engagements while managing and mentoring a team of six designers (Middle to Lead). People leadership isn't new territory — I've been managing designers since 2019, running 1-on-1s, assessments, salary reviews, and development planning at Andersen Lab.

I've led discovery and strategy for healthcare ecosystems with Johnson & Johnson, Janssen, and Sandoz; defined AI-driven experiences showcased at Google Cloud Next; modernized operational platforms for Halliburton, Schlumberger, and Kongsberg; and designed financial products for Paxful, AWS/Omantel, and Interswitch.
