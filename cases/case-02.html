Work / Case 02
Delivery

Paxful — a global peer-to-peer crypto marketplace

Role
Senior UX Designer → Lead Mobile UX
Domain
FinTech / Crypto
Timeline
2020–2021
Scope
Web, iOS & Android · Africa, Asia, USA, Europe
Context

A financial passport for users worldwide: 300+ payment methods connecting buyers and sellers. The task — “gold” solutions that work across radically different markets and cultures, with one cohesive UX across web, mobile web, and native apps.

Approach
  • UX research with Smartlook, Attention Insights, and desk research; journey maps, personas, and user tests.
  • Led key features across desktop and mobile: Trade History, Create an Offer, Trade Dashboard, Marketplace.
  • Built and documented a cross-functional design system — 40+ Figma pages with design tokens, atomic structure, auto-layouts, and variants.
Outcomes

Business value
  • Audience growth: research-led regional adaptations increased target audience and engagement across four markets.
  • Acquisition & retention: 4★+ App Store / Google Play ratings strengthened trust and organic acquisition.
  • Dev-cost reduction: the token-based design system streamlined development and cut cross-platform inconsistencies.
