Work / Case 03
Delivery

Industrial IoT — factory control desktop app

Role
Senior UX/UI Designer
Domain
Industrial IoT
Timeline
2019
Scope
Automation & digitalisation for process manufacturing
Context

Intelligent infrastructure for manufacturing, rail transport, and digital healthcare. The hardest constraint: end users on the factory floor had little digital experience, so every insight had to be earned through research and translated into radically simple design.

Approach
  • Deep user interviews and testing with factory workers — 70+ questions per research cycle, personas per user segment.
  • Stakeholder interviews established clear role distribution and realistic project stages.
  • Sketches and low-fi prototypes validated hypotheses before high-fidelity investment; then a “turn-on and go” real-time UI.
Outcomes
91%
efficiency increase measured after redesign
70+
interview questions per research cycle
Business value
  • Productivity gain: a 91% measured efficiency increase translated directly into operational-cost savings.
  • Lower training cost: a “turn-on and go” UI made the system usable with minimal onboarding.
  • Faster decisions: real-time visibility enabled data-driven decision-making without losing process details.
