Work / Case 06
Delivery

Premium fashion e-commerce platform

Role
Senior UX/UI Designer
Domain
Retail / E-commerce
Timeline
2018–2019
Scope
Desktop & mobile web · data-driven UX
Context

A destination for the world's leading fashion brands — authenticity and excellence as the core promise. The brief demanded premium UX/UI quality, direct customer-feedback loops informing continuous improvement, and a fully responsive mobile experience.

Approach
  • In-depth user interviews (40+ questions) revealed shopping motivations, frustrations, and discovery habits.
  • Design-driven approach: hypotheses validated with user tests; analytics platforms integrated for measurable decisions.
  • Heatmap analytics visualised real interaction patterns, driving data-informed refinements to journeys and layouts.

Business value
  • Conversion uplift: heatmap-driven refinements measurably improved conversion rates and engagement.
  • Brand trust: a premium, authenticity-first experience elevated customer confidence.
  • Reduced guesswork: analytics-informed decisions replaced opinion with evidence, lowering redesign waste.
