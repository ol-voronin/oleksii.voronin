Internal mobile app for a home-improvement retailer

Role
Senior UX Architect
Domain
Retail
Timeline
Pre-sale
Scope
Pre-sale concept · retail chain with 35,000+ employees
Context

A leading home-improvement and gardening retail chain needed to replace its outdated retail management system. I designed the concept for a custom, easy-to-use mobile web application that automates and digitises business processes, streamlines sales-floor and back-office tasks, and lets employees work from anywhere in the store.

Approach
  • Concept for one tool covering sales floor and back office.
  • Work-from-anywhere-in-store flows for 35,000+ employees.
  • Process digitisation mapped to daily staff tasks.
Outcomes
35K+
employees in the retail chain
1
tool replacing the legacy system
Business value
  • Staff-hours saved daily: one tool for the floor and back office across a 35,000-employee chain.
  • Modernisation without disruption: flows designed to replace the legacy system without operational risk.
