Work / Case 04
Delivery

Interswitch — 12 banking apps for Africa's leading fintech

Role
Senior UX/UI Designer
Domain
FinTech / Banking
Timeline
2019–2020
Scope
Mobile banking · 4 African regions
Context

Interswitch's payment platform shaped the Nigerian payments landscape and powers its expansion across Africa. My work: designs for 12 bank apps, then a white-label template making every future bank launch dramatically cheaper and faster — while respecting regional cultures, regulatory frameworks, and security.

Approach
  • UX audit, stakeholder and user interviews, proto-personas, and a unified workflow for the white-label template.
  • Research-driven information architecture and high-fidelity wireframes, validated with user tests.
  • Consolidated per-app UI kits into one general design system with internal kits — unifying approaches and cutting costs.
Outcomes
12
banking applications delivered
4
African regions, culturally adapted
1
white-label template for all future apps
9+
additional apps launched on the template
Business value
  • Compounding ROI: the white-label template cut cost and time of every subsequent bank launch — 9+ further apps shipped on it.
  • Reduced development cost: one consolidated design system decreased build and maintenance spend.
  • Risk & trust: security patterns designed around real regional user behaviour protected users and brand reputation.
