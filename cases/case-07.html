Career Flow concept for an Oil & Gas world leader

Role
Senior UX/UI Designer
Domain
Energy / HR Tech
Timeline
Pre-sale
Scope
Pre-sale concept · global energy technology provider
Context

For the world's leading provider of reservoir characterisation, drilling, and production technology (90,000 employees, 120+ countries), I built a comprehensive pre-sale concept for a “Career Flow” interface — an intuitive, engaging experience guiding employees through their career development journey, from initial exploration to growth paths.

Approach
  • Concept design for the full career journey across desktop and mobile.
  • Guided step flows: qualification → skills → experience → matched opportunities.
  • Wireframes to polished UI, packaged as a pitch-ready narrative for client stakeholders.
